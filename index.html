Good to know

Questions, answered.

Offer rules and reward options can vary. Check the details in Freecash before getting started.

How does Freecash work?

Browse the activities available to you, review an offer’s requirements, and follow its instructions. Any eligible reward depends on successful completion and verification under the offer’s terms.

Is a specific reward amount guaranteed?

No. Reward amounts and availability vary by offer and region. Participation does not guarantee a particular amount of rewards or income.

Are offers available everywhere?

No. Offers and eligibility can differ by country, account, and partner. Check the app for the activities currently available to you.

What payout options can I use?

Available reward and cash-out options can vary by region and account. See the current options and any applicable requirements in Freecash.

Should I check offer terms first?

Yes. Review the requirements, deadlines, eligibility rules, and any costs or subscriptions before starting an offer.
