02 / Process

How tracking and verification work

Tasks on Freecash are run with external research providers, game studios, and partner brands. Keep these three habits so completions credit reliably.

Step 01

Choose an eligible offer

Browse the catalog filtered for your country and OS. App and game campaigns almost always require a first-time installation on your device.

Step 02

Read the milestone & timer

Every listing states the target level or action, how many days you have to finish, and whether an optional purchase or trial is involved.

Step 03

Launch directly & cash out

Start the offer straight from Freecash with VPNs and ad-blockers off so attribution stays intact, then withdraw verified rewards in your region.

04 / FAQ

Common questions

Direct answers on payouts, regional catalogs, and where to get help with this page.

Is a specific reward amount guaranteed?

No. Available tasks and reward rates depend on your country and partner demand, and payouts require verified completion under each offer’s rules.

Why do offers differ depending on where I live?

Survey providers and game studios set regional budgets, languages, and target devices, so you only see offers currently active for your location.

Should I create an account before installing the app?

Yes — registering first makes signing into the app immediate after installation. Use the Create free account button at the top of the page.

What cash-out options are supported?

Withdrawal methods, minimum thresholds, and processing speeds vary by region. Check the cashout tab inside Freecash for the options enabled on your account.

How do I report a broken link or ask about this site?

Open an issue on the deve-pro/deve-pro.github.io GitHub repository and include your device and browser details.
