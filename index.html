How it works

Every task on Freecash is run with an external survey provider, game studio, or partner service. Stick to three habits so your progress tracks properly.

Step 01

Pick an eligible offer

Browse the catalog shown for your country and device. Game and app offers usually apply to first-time installs only.

Step 02

Read the terms before starting

Check the target level or task, the time window to finish, and whether a purchase or subscription is required.

Step 03

Keep tracking intact & redeem

Launch the offer directly from Freecash without VPNs or ad blockers. Once verified, redeem through the payout options in your region.

Common questions

Straight answers on payouts, regional availability, and support.

Is a specific reward amount guaranteed?

No. Available tasks and reward rates vary by country and partner demand, and payouts require verified completion under each offer’s rules.

Why do offers differ depending on where I live?

Advertisers and survey researchers target specific countries, languages, and devices. You will only see offers currently open to your region and account.

Should I create an account before installing the app?

Yes — registering your account first makes signing into the app immediate after installation. Use the Create free account link at the top of the page.

What cash-out options are available?

Withdrawal methods, minimum thresholds, and processing times vary by region. Check the cashout tab inside Freecash for the options enabled on your account.

How do I report a broken download link or ask about this site?

Open an issue on the deve-pro/deve-pro.github.io GitHub repository and include your device and browser details.
